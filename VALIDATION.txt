LOGIC AUDIT FINDINGS
- V0.7.6 correctly made the deterministic engine authoritative, but the strongest direction was selected for every game. With any non-zero model disagreement, that could leave zero PASS games. V0.7.7 adds a 30%-of-threshold noise floor so trivial differences remain PASS.
- V0.7.6 thresholds (NFL spread 1.5 / total 2.0) were permissive relative to the small recent-form sample. V0.7.7 raises NFL to spread 2.0 / total 2.5.
- CFB currently has only recent scoring/margin context and is NOT explicitly opponent-strength adjusted. Rather than pretending otherwise, V0.7.7 anchors CFB more strongly to the verified DraftKings market and requires larger edges (spread 2.5 / total 3.0).
- These guardrails are conservative engineering thresholds, not statistically proven optimal betting thresholds. Historical out-of-sample performance is still required before claiming calibration.
