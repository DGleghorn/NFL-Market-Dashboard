SCOPE
V0.8.0 is the first substantive CFB model upgrade after the NFL freeze. NFL remains on football-077-frozen. CFB now builds a season-to-date opponent graph from completed ESPN games and uses it for spread fair values before conservative DraftKings market shrinkage. PGA remains shadow/fail-closed.

CFB OPPONENT MODEL
- Completed regular-season games from Week 1 through the week before the target slate.
- Margin normalized for 2.5 points of home field.
- Game margin capped at +/-35 to reduce blowout distortion.
- 24-iteration opponent graph estimates schedule-adjusted team strength.
- Small-sample shrinkage is applied inside the rating graph.
- CFB spread fair value uses opponent-adjusted rating differential + HFA when both teams have valid ratings.
- CFB totals remain scoring-profile based and market-shrunk; they are NOT represented as opponent-adjusted.
- Verified DraftKings remains the only actionable sportsbook.
